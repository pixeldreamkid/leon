# Daily plan

Help the owner plan today.

1. Gather today's tasks. Use what the owner gave you, or check their connected task list, calendar and notes. If nothing is available, ask them for today's tasks.
2. Pick the three highest-impact priorities.
3. List quick wins that take under 15 minutes.
4. List items blocked on other people, with who each one is waiting on.

Reply in a short plain list with three headings: Priorities, Quick wins, Waiting on. No extra commentary.
